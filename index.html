Ready, Maths Explorer?

Help Pip the explorer cross the colourful world. Collect ⭐ stars and hit golden question blocks to answer Year 7 maths questions.

Keyboard: ← → or A/D to move · ↑ or W or SPACE to jump

Topics: integers, factors and multiples, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratio, algebra, and angles.

How to play: Move with the keyboard, jump onto platforms, collect stars, and touch a golden ? block to answer a question. Each correct answer earns 10 stars. Reach the flag to finish the level!
Teacher note: This is an original classroom game inspired by classic side-scrolling platform adventures; it does not use official Super Mario characters or assets.
